
# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful and welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of age, background, experience, or identity.

## Our Standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate.
- Welcoming different opinions and ideas.
- Giving constructive feedback.
- Accepting responsibility for mistakes.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

Examples include harassment, discrimination, offensive comments, personal attacks, and disrespectful behaviour.

## Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces and public interactions related to this project.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the project maintainers.
